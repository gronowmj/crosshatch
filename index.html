        How scoring works

        Every puzzle starts at 100 points. Help costs points; time never does.

        • Reveals only charge for letters they fill in or correct. If your letters were already right, it costs the same as a check instead.
        • Revealed and confirmed letters are never charged twice, and a check with nothing new to check is free.
        • A plain clue adds a straight, quick-crossword definition under the cryptic clue. It’s charged once per clue, stays shown, and isn’t offered for a word you’ve already solved.
        • Wrong entries cost nothing, and neither does the glow when you complete a word correctly.
        • The score can’t go below 0, and clearing the grid or reloading doesn’t restore points.
